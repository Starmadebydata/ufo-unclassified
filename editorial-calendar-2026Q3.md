# UFO Declassified — 3-Month Editorial Calendar (July–September 2026)

## Strategy Summary

- **Frequency**: 1 deep analysis (2000-3000 words) + 1 short analysis (800-1200 words) per week = ~2 posts/week, 26 posts total in 3 months. The "short" post can flex to a Release 04 rapid-response when a new tranche drops.
- **Keyword approach**: No keyword density targeting. Each article targets one **search intent** and naturally covers 5-10 related entities. Target phrase appears in H1, first paragraph, and 1-2 H2s — never forced beyond that.
- **Content pillars** (rotate to build topical authority):
  1. **Historical deep dives** (pre-2000 cases from CIA/FBI/DOS files)
  2. **Modern military encounters** (post-2000 DOW files with video evidence)
  3. **Government/institutional analysis** (AARO, Congress, CIA panels, policy)
  4. **Methodology & guides** (how to read files, citation, research tools)
- **Internal linking**: Every article links to 2-3 existing articles + 2-3 document pages. This builds topical authority clusters.
- **Differentiation**: Every article has a clear judgment or unique angle in the title. Not "X file analysis" but "X: what the file shows AND what it's missing."

## SEO Notes Per Article

For each article, the on-page structure should be:

```
H1: [Target phrase naturally embedded] — [Unique judgment]
Opening paragraph: Answer "what is this and why should I care" in 100 words. Target phrase appears once.
H2: What the [file/report/video] Actually Shows
H2: What's Missing From the Record
H2: How This Compares to [Related case]  ← internal link
H2: Common Questions About [topic]
  H3: [PAA question 1]
  H3: [PAA question 2]
  H3: [PAA question 3]
H2: Source Files and Further Reading  ← links to document pages
```

Check Google "People Also Ask" for each topic before writing and answer those questions verbatim as H3s. This is more effective than any keyword density strategy.

## Tracking

After publishing each article:
1. Submit URL in Google Search Console (URL Inspection → Request Indexing)
2. Check Search Console performance weekly for impressions/CTR on new articles
3. After 4 weeks, review which queries are bringing impressions — those are your real keywords (not what you predicted)
4. Adjust future topics based on what's actually getting impressions
